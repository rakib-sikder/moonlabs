# Moonlab image prompts

One prompt per image slot on moonlab.space. Each prompt is complete: copy one whole block into Gemini and generate.

## How to use

1. **Open a new Gemini chat for every image.** In the same chat Gemini keeps editing the previous picture, so you get the same image again.
2. **Do not attach any earlier image.** The style is already written inside every prompt.
3. Paste one whole block and generate. Save the result under the file name given, into `public/images/`.
4. If Gemini adds any writing, a person or a hand, reply: `Remove all text and people. Every surface is blank paper.`
5. If the amber spreads everywhere, reply: `Use amber only on the one object named in the scene. Everything else stays graphite.`
6. If one image comes out in a different look from the rest, regenerate it in another new chat.

## Do not generate these

- **Founder photo, client logos, testimonial photos:** use real ones only.
- **Product screenshots** (LeadPilot, the client portal): use real screenshots.

## 01. Social share image

- **File:** `public/images/og-cover.jpg`
- **Shape:** 16:9, then crop to 1200x630
- **Used on:** Link previews on Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp. The site title is added on top in code.

```
Create a brand-new image. Aspect ratio: 16:9. High resolution.

THE SCENE (this is the subject, and it is the most important part): A small paper desk seen from the front-right. On the right third of the frame: a neat stack of small blank paper notes sitting squared-up inside a shallow paper tray, a tiny paper desk lamp with an amber paper shade bending over it, and one last loose note leaning against the tray with a single short amber paper strip across it like a strike-through. The lamp shade is the amber light source. Composition: all objects stay in the right 40% of the frame; the left 60% is empty, dark, softly graded backdrop with no objects at all, reserved for a headline.

HOW IT IS MADE AND LIT (the look only, this adds no extra objects): A photorealistic macro studio photograph of a handmade paper-craft miniature diorama. Every object in the scene is cut, scored and folded by hand from thick matte paper and card. The paper is deep graphite and charcoal (between #0b0c0f and #272b33), with visible paper fibre, crisp knife-cut edges, tiny glue seams and soft layered shadows between the layers. The diorama sits on a dark matte charcoal desk surface at night, against a plain near-black backdrop (#0b0c0f) that fades to darkness at the edges. There is exactly one accent colour: warm amber (#f5b24e), used only where described below, like lamplight. Somewhere in the upper part of the frame hangs one small crescent moon cut from warm off-white paper (#ece6d8) on an almost invisible thread; it is small and quiet, never the main subject. Lighting: a single small warm amber light source inside the scene, plus a very faint cool fill so the dark paper shapes stay readable. Gentle glow, long soft shadows, no harsh highlights. Camera: 85mm macro lens at f/4, slightly raised three-quarter view, shallow depth of field with the main subject tack sharp and the background softly blurred. Calm, tidy, quiet mood: a workplace late at night where the work finishes by itself and nobody is there. Strictly no people, no hands, no faces, no animals. No text, letters, numbers, logos, brand marks, screens, user-interface elements or watermarks anywhere; every sheet, sign, card and label is blank. Not a 3D render, not a cartoon, not an illustration, not neon, not glossy plastic: it must look like real paper photographed in a studio.

The only objects in the picture are the ones listed in THE SCENE, plus the small paper moon.
```
