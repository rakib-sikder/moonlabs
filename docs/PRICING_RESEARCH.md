## Rules for the AI

1. Vague requirements: widen the range by 30-50%.
2. Over about $15,000, unclear scope, health or finance data, more than 3 systems, or systems
   without an API: no price on screen; hand over to a person (paid discovery $1,500-5,000).
3. Rush (faster than two thirds of the normal time): +25-50%.
4. Always say what is not included: hosting, tool subscriptions (n8n, Zapier, GoHighLevel),
   SMS and AI usage.
5. Fixed price under $15,000; milestones above it with 20-30% upfront. 60-90 day warranty.
6. Plan fit: $99 covers one very small task; $199/month light care of 1-5 flows; $399/month a
   managed service line.
